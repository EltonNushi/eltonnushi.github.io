Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 23, 2026

Welcome to TableMaster Pro, a mobile software application designed and operated by SwiftTech Mobile ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to upholding absolute data privacy, transparency, and data sovereignty for all restaurant operators and end-users.

🛡️ Data Sovereignty Clause: SwiftTech Mobile does not own, harvest, monetize, or store your restaurant's guest logs, customer databases, or reservation histories on any private third-party corporate servers.

1. The Architecture of Data Sovereignty

TableMaster Pro is structurally built as an decentralized, offline-first application. Your operational restaurant data—including reservation sheets, floor blueprint maps, client names, phone records, VIP designations, and allergen metrics—lives strictly inside an isolated on-device sandbox sandbox via Apple's native SwiftData caching framework [INDEX].

2. Cloud Synchronization and Encryption

When you utilize multi-device live synchronization or the online website reservation widget token rules, data transmission skips external server hubs completely [INDEX]. Instead, synchronization loops use Apple's native iCloud and CloudKit database infrastructure [INDEX].

  • Data is fully encrypted both in transit and at rest using your corporate Apple ID credentials.
  • SwiftTech Mobile maintains no backend APIs or database tunnels capable of reading, accessing, or intercepting this network stream.
  • Your guest list remains completely hidden from external corporate data miners and advertising pipelines [INDEX].

3. Data Portability and Erasure

Because you maintain complete ownership of your database ledger, you retain un-bypassable control over its portability [INDEX]:

  • Data Export: You can instantly generate highly structured, RFC-compliant CSV text data logs from your dashboard workspace to export your client arrays to external spreadsheets (e.g., Excel, Google Sheets) anytime.
  • Data Deletion: Deleting the TableMaster Pro application from your master iOS device or formatting your iCloud container removes your entire customer ledger registry instantly and permanently. We maintain no server backups to recover data once wiped.

4. App Store Subscription E-Commerce Data

Our monthly subscription monetization processing is regulated securely via Apple's native StoreKit 2 framework [INDEX]. Financial transactions, 14-day free trials, and renewal tracking passes are managed entirely by Apple Inc. [INDEX] We never collect, process, or view credit card statements, bank routing codes, or billing addresses. Your payment history loops are protected under Apple’s global privacy terms [INDEX].

5. Changes to This Privacy Document

We reserve the right to modify this operational privacy policy to maintain compliance with updated App Store Review Guidelines or local international data storage regulations.
