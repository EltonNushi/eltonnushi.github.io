Log Data and Crash Diagnostics
We want to inform you that whenever an unexpected runtime error, system crash, or syntax freeze occurs within any of our applications, diagnostic technical data called Log Data may be compiled anonymously via local system frameworks. This diagnostic package contains your hardware device model, operating system software version, the structural configuration state of the application at the precise millisecond of the failure event, and other basic technical performance statistics. This payload is stored locally or transmitted anonymously to Apple, utilized entirely to engineer software stability patches.

Cookies and Local Device Memory Storage
Cookies are files with a small amount of data that are commonly used as anonymous unique identifiers. These are sent to your browser from the websites that you visit and are stored on your device's internal memory.

While our native iOS applications operate completely independent of tracking browser cookies, our files utilize encrypted local SQLite database partitions directly on your internal hardware memory to maintain your active layouts, calculations, and properties across device power cycles. This service does not use these “cookies” explicitly. However, the apps may use third-party runtime development libraries that use localized caching to improve asset performance.
