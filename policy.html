Information Collection and Use

For a premium on-device experience, while using our apps, your generated database entries—including inventory sheets, time clocks, flashcards, and table blueprint configurations—are retained strictly within your device's isolated local storage sandbox [INDEX]. This data is completely private and is not collected by the developer in any way [INDEX]. For apps that feature automated background backups or multi-device live syncing (such as HoursTracker, StockKeeper, and TableMaster Pro), database files are synchronized exclusively using Apple's end-to-end encrypted native iCloud and CloudKit infrastructure [INDEX]. We maintain no middleman server pipelines to intercept this secure network stream [INDEX].

Log Data & Analytics

We want to inform you that our native iOS and macOS applications do not collect your personal telemetry data, internet protocol (IP) addresses, or usage statistics. In the event of a system crash, crash diagnostics logs are generated entirely through Apple's native, anonymised App Store crash reporting systems to provide service improvements only if you have opted-in through your global iOS privacy settings.

Cookies

Cookies are small tracking files commonly utilized as anonymous unique identifiers on web browsers. Our mobile applications execute natively on Apple platforms and do not use cookies explicitly to monitor or analyze user behaviors [INDEX].

Financial and Subscription Security

All commercial in-app features, receipt restorations, and auto-renewable monthly memberships are processed securely via Apple's native StoreKit 2 framework [INDEX]. SwiftTech Mobile never views, collects, or stores any banking data, credit card information, routing numbers, or billing locations [INDEX]. Financial transaction security loops are managed entirely by Apple Inc. under their global consumer protection standards [INDEX].

Children’s Privacy

Because our applications strictly collect zero personally identifiable information from any end-user, they comply fully with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly target, track, or manage data for children under the age of 13.

Data Portability and Erasure

You maintain absolute ownership and control over your data portability loops at all times [INDEX]. Deleting a SwiftTech Mobile application from your iOS device or formatting your private iCloud container removes your entire customer ledger registry instantly and permanently [INDEX]. We maintain no background cloud archives to recover user data once wiped from the device [INDEX].
