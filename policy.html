Information Collection and Operational Data Use
Depending on the specific SwiftTech Mobile utility initialized by your establishment, our software engines process specific operational datasets required to manage your day-to-day hospitality infrastructure:
• Guest and Reservation Data (TableMaster Suite): Captures customer names, booking dates, party numbers, seating layout allocations, phone contacts, and allergen profiles to construct your visual timeline ledger.
• Culinary & Menu Schemas (MenuForge): Stores local recipe parameters, price tiers, inventory tracking metrics, and digital menu layout grids.
• Workforce Logistics (StaffPulse): Maintains staff shift details, hours configurations, supervisor PIN assignments, and payroll calculation tracking rows.
• Financial Metadata (BillFlow): Localizes tax matrices, checkout receipts, invoice tallies, and daily checkout ledger balances.

Critical Privacy Guard: All underlying operational business records, logs, configuration data, and manager security PINs are kept strictly localized inside your device hardware sandbox partition or securely mirrored within your personal, encrypted iCloud private database containers. The developer does not harvest, intercept, or maintain external server access to your restaurant's internal data snapshots.

Log Data and Crash Diagnostics
We want to inform you that whenever an unexpected runtime error, syntax freeze, or system crash occurs within any of our applications, diagnostic technical data called Log Data may be compiled anonymously. This diagnostic package contains your hardware device model, operating system software version, the structural configuration state of the application at the precise millisecond of the failure event, and other basic technical performance statistics. This payload is utilized entirely to engineer system stability patches and optimize software compilation passes.

Cookies and Local Device Memory Storage
Cookies are files with a small amount of data that are commonly used as anonymous unique identifiers. These are sent to your browser from the websites that you visit and are stored on your device's internal memory.

While our native iOS applications operate completely independent of tracking browser cookies, our files utilize encrypted local SQLite database partitions directly on your internal hardware memory to maintain your configuration settings, shift logs, and active layout structures across device power cycles. This service does not use these “cookies” explicitly. However, the app may use third-party code and libraries that use “cookies” to collect information and improve their services.
